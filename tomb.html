Game Project: Tomb of the Saintess



This game was created by Stormie Moore.





Gallery


This is a short walkthrough of the walking simulator, with lights and sounds on to show the atmosphere of the game. The ending shows the inside of the tomb itself, and the photogrammatry object I created for the project- my personal jounal.




This section shows the first area the player encounters in the walking simulator. The way down for the player is marked by rocky outcrops and giant boulders. They are spaced evenly to allow the player to jump down, but not to get back up. One technique I used to add realism to the scene was taking a cylinder disk into the Unreal model editor and using the sculpting/noise tools to make a uneven surface- simulating leaf litter from the plants above the drop down into the cave. This is the same technique I used to create the mossy ground textures seen throughout the walking simulator. The final image has the late afternoon lighting and glowing mushrooms to add to the mysterious atmosphere, along with helping the player see the path forward.




This section is of the exit of the cave tunnel, leading into the enclosed clearing within the mountains. I wanted there to be a natural slope up from the cave tunnel to the tomb. Here, you can see the worn and crumbling front gate that had once blocked the entrance to the tomb, and the start of the atmospheric fog seeping out of the next area. The old stump with a new tree growing out of it represents the passage of time for the area and how nature has been slowly taking over. The free asset vines hanging from the top of the tunnel exit and edges of the clearing were added to soften the hard edges of the rock. The entire area is meant to feel quiet and peaceful.




This is the main area of the walking simulator, the tomb entrance. This place was inspired by carved cliff tombs in Persia and the Middle East. I wanted to add more greenery to this section, to drive home the idea that this entire place has been abandoned by people and overtaken by nature. The gates were given rusted metal textures, with one of the free asset statues being broken. I added many small shrubs and leaf litter models to add as clutter around the clearing. The atmospheric fog is settled in the center of this area, almost like it poured out from the now opened tomb. Lanerns are used in this area to help the player see more clearly, but also to provide contrasting light to the afternoon sun peeking over the clearing's edge.
