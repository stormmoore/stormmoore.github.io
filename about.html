About Me




My name is Stormie Moore. I am a current student at Boise State University, pursuing a Bachelors degree in Games, Interactive Media, and Mobile Technology. I have always been interested in games, but more specifically the art withing those games. Art is a passion of mine, and has been for years. Being able to express yourself through different mediums and tell engaging stories with your works is what I love about art. My dream is to one day make my own games that inspire others the way I have been inspired! Some of my favorite games include Kingdoms of Amalur: Reckoning, The Mass Effect Trilogy, OneShot, and Spiritfarer.

Other than playing games, I also enjoy drawing, journaling, and learning new mediums to create art- one of which is 3D modeling.




My Experience and Skills



Experience

I have experience working with a variety of different mediums, including: traditional art, digital art, 3D modeling, and game development. I have worked on multiple game projects, both solo and with teams as school assignments, and have created a variety of different art pieces. This includes both personal works and commissions over the last 5 years.

Skills

My skills include: 2D art, 3D modeling, character design, environmental design, and game development. I am proficient in a variety of different software programs, including: Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Animate, Figma, Clip Studio Paint, Unity, and Unreal Engine.
